## Operating principles

The three concepts that govern most of Avery's decisions:

- [[Boring Stack First]]: Postgres + a single Python service beats five
  trendy ones at this team size.
- [[Provenance Over Recall]]: every claim cites a source; we trust
  systems that show their work.
- [[Small Teams Compounding]]: the org chart is the architecture; protect
  the team's ability to ship in days, not quarters.
