# Should I bring up the VP Eng hire with Linh?

A saved deliberation, written by [[Avery Chen]] after a Friday-evening
walk on 2026-05-22. The question: should Avery surface
[[Theo Nakamura]] as a real candidate to [[Linh Park]] now, or wait
until the role is scoped and Theo is more reachable?

## The honest version of the question

The version of this question Avery doesn't say out loud: "do I want
Theo *and* do I want to be the kind of founding engineer who proactively
recruits the person who will be senior to me?" Both have to be true
for the move to make sense.

## What this wiki says

Three pages bear on the answer:

- [[Theo Nakamura]]: the candidate exists and is plausibly
  pace-matched, but the relationship is warm-not-deep and Theo's
  current role is not yet visibly broken. Status: tracking, not
  pursuing.
- [[2026-04-30 Keep Team Onsite]]: Theo is currently remote.
  Either the conversation waits until Theo would relocate, or the
  policy bends, or Theo isn't the hire. The most likely answer is
  "the conversation waits 6-12 months."
- [[2026-05-20 Postpone Series A]]: the Series A timing was just
  postponed by 9-12 months. The natural moment for a VP Eng hire is
  *after* the Series A, not before. Bringing Theo to Linh now creates
  pressure to either (a) hire ahead of capital or (b) explicitly
  defer a relationship Theo has not been told to expect. Neither is
  clean.

## Tentative answer

**Don't surface Theo to Linh yet.** Three concrete reasons:

1. The role hasn't been scoped. Bringing a named candidate to a
   founder-CEO before scoping the role is the [[Mia Patel]] pattern
   inverted. For Mia, the role was clear and the candidates were
   the variable. For VP Eng, the role itself is the variable.
2. The constraint stack has just shifted twice in two weeks
   ([[2026-04-30 Keep Team Onsite]], [[2026-05-20 Postpone Series A]]).
   Adding a named-candidate conversation on top is asking
   [[Linh Park]] to re-plan with new information she hasn't asked
   for.
3. The cost of waiting six months is approximately zero. Theo is
   not actively interviewing. The two-coffees cadence can continue
   without escalating to a formal process.

## What to do this week instead

- Draft a one-pager titled "When do we need a VP Eng?": explicitly
  no candidates named, just the question of timing and shape.
- Bring the one-pager to a Friday 1:1 with [[Linh Park]] *not* on
  [[Demo Friday]]. This is not a demo-table conversation.
- If Linh wants to talk about candidates after reading the one-pager,
  *then* Theo's name comes up. Not before.

## Risk

The risk in waiting: Theo's current role might stabilize and they
might re-anchor where they are. Acknowledged. The mitigation is the
existing two-coffees cadence. Keep the relationship warm, don't
let it go cold over the next quarter.
