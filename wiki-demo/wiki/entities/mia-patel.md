# Mia Patel

Founding designer at [[Strand Bio]]. Hired May 2026 after a three-week
interview process (see [[2026-05-02 Hire Founding Designer]]). Previously
designed for a series-B developer-tools startup; before that, product
design at a consumer health app.

## Why they were hired

Three signals stood out:

1. Could read the [[Strand Bio Inventory Service]] codebase
   well enough to critique the data model in their second interview.
   Design candidates who can read code are rare and load-bearing for a
   small team. See [[Small Teams Compounding]].
2. Had strong opinions about provenance UX. The case study they walked
   through showed every claim in their interface cited the source row in
   the underlying DB. Aligned with [[Provenance Over Recall]].
3. Visibly preferred boring tools (Figma + a handwritten state-machine
   diagram) over a portfolio of process buzzwords. Aligned with
   [[Boring Stack First]].

## Current focus

Owns the v1 design for the inventory service and the next two product
surfaces after that. Pairs with [[Avery Chen]] weekly on the same demo
cadence as the rest of the team.
