# Avery Chen

Founding engineer at [[Strand Bio]]. Background: 6 years at a series-A
medtech startup, two years before that at a research lab building software
for high-throughput screening. Joined Strand Bio at month 3, employee #2
after the CEO.

## What they own

- The full backend (Python, FastAPI, Postgres)
- Infrastructure (Fly.io + Cloudflare)
- All technical hiring decisions

## How they work

Avery is opinionated about [[Boring Stack First]] and skeptical of any
framework introduced in the last 18 months. Sees [[Provenance Over Recall]]
as the difference between a debuggable system and an unmaintainable one,
and treats it as a hiring filter. Candidates who can't explain *why* a
decision was made rarely scale past the founding team.

Operates on a "weekly demo Friday" cadence with [[Mia Patel]] and the CEO.
Believes [[Small Teams Compounding]] is the only real moat at this stage.
