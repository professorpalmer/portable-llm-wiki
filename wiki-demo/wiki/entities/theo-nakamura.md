# Theo Nakamura

Engineering-leadership candidate [[Avery Chen]] is informally
recruiting toward a future VP Engineering role at [[Strand Bio]].
**Not yet in an active hiring process**. See
[[Should I bring up the VP Eng hire with Linh?]] for the deliberation.

## Background

Twelve years of engineering, of which the last five were as Director
of Engineering at a series-B health-tech startup that grew from 14 to
60 engineers under their watch. Before that: backend infra at a large
genomics company, where Theo and [[Avery Chen]] worked on adjacent
teams for eighteen months.

The connection is warm but not deep. Three coffees over four years,
plus a long thread on Signal about scaling engineering culture past
the founding team.

## Why Avery is tracking them

Three reasons, ordered by weight:

1. **Pace match.** Theo has run the 9→30 engineer transition before,
   which is the transition [[Strand Bio]] will hit in 12–18 months
   if the current trajectory holds. Most engineering-leadership
   candidates Avery has talked to have either run 0→9 (too early) or
   30→100 (too late). Theo's last role was exactly the right slice.
2. **Stack-fit on hiring.** In the Signal thread Theo independently
   articulated something close to [[Small Teams Compounding]]
   without prompting. The same instinct to optimize for fewer,
   higher-leverage hires rather than headcount growth as a vanity
   metric.
3. **Available-ish.** Theo's current role is going through a
   reorg and the equity refresh window closed in February. Soft
   signal, but worth tracking.

## Risk

Avery has not had this conversation with [[Linh Park]] yet. Bringing
a named candidate into the founder-CEO conversation before the role
has been scoped is the kind of move Linh will reasonably push back on.
The right sequence is: scope first, candidate-mine second.

## Status

Two coffees logged. No formal interview process. Avery has not
revealed that there is a potential role on the horizon.
