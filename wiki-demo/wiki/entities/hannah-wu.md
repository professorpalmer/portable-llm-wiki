# Hannah Wu

Dr. Hannah Wu. [[Avery Chen]]'s former PhD advisor at Stanford
(2018–2021, before Avery left the program. See
[[2024-08-15 Leaving Grad School]]). Tenured faculty in computational
biology, runs a 12-person lab focused on protein-structure prediction
and active-learning methods for wet-lab experiments.

## Relationship today

Civil but not close. Hannah supported Avery's departure in writing
(the recommendation letter to Avery's first industry job is in
[[2024-08-15 Leaving Grad School]] as a quoted excerpt) but was
visibly disappointed at the time. They have a standing twice-yearly
coffee when Avery is in the Bay Area.

Hannah is a useful *strategic* sounding board. Particularly on
hiring-from-academia questions. And a deliberately distant
*tactical* one. Avery doesn't bring product-roadmap questions to
Hannah; she does bring questions like "is this candidate likely to
flame out at month nine?" and gets unvarnished answers.

## Why she matters to this wiki

Hannah is the most visible counterargument-shaped person in Avery's
life: the version of Avery who stayed in academia would be at her
lab now. Decisions that touch *long-horizon vs. short-horizon
thinking* almost always involve a tacit "what would Hannah do?"
check. Especially in
[[2026-05-20 Postpone Series A]] and the broader question of how
long [[Strand Bio]] should stay small (see
[[Small Teams Compounding]]).
