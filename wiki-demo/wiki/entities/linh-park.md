# Linh Park

CEO and cofounder of [[Strand Bio]]. Biologist by training. PhD in
protein engineering from MIT (2018), postdoc at the Broad Institute
(2018–2022). Spun Strand Bio out of an unfunded side-project during the
last six months of the postdoc.

## How Linh works

The default mode is **biologist-first product manager**: every product
discussion starts by asking "what would have made my own postdoc 30%
faster?" before any abstract market analysis. Holds the company's
opinion on what counts as a real customer (a working scientist with a
broken Excel file) and what doesn't (a director-of-platform-engineering
running a procurement RFP).

Strict about [[Provenance Over Recall]] for the same reason
[[Avery Chen]] is. Linh has been on the wrong end of un-cited claims
during PhD defenses and learned to never trust a number she can't
trace.

## Relationship with Avery

Linh recruited Avery directly off a cold email in late 2025
(see [[2025-09-14 Joining Strand Bio]]). The working model is that Linh
owns *what to build* and Avery owns *how it ships*, with overlap
arbitrated on Friday (see [[Demo Friday]]).

Disagreements so far have all been about pace, not direction. Linh
wants to wait for evidence before hiring; Avery wants to hire one role
ahead of pain. The compromise has held. See
[[2026-05-02 Hire Founding Designer]] (Avery's pace) versus
[[2026-05-20 Postpone Series A]] (Linh's pace).
