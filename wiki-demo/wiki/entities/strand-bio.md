# Strand Bio

Boston-based synthetic-biology startup. Team of 9 (as of May 2026), seed
round closed in late 2025. Building lab-automation software for
protein-engineering groups inside academic labs and early-stage biotechs.

## Product

The core product is a workflow orchestrator that sits between LIMS (lab
information management systems) and the wet-lab hardware (liquid handlers,
plate readers, incubators). Replaces a stack of brittle Excel macros and
hand-edited Python scripts.

Current shipping focus: [[Strand Bio Inventory Service]], the v1 of a
reagent + plate inventory tracker.

## Why it might work

The wet-lab automation tooling market is small but capital-rich, and the
existing incumbents (Benchling, LabKey) are too horizontal to serve a
protein-engineering team natively. Strand Bio's bet is that vertical
specialization beats horizontal coverage at this team size. The
[[Small Teams Compounding]] argument applied to product strategy.
