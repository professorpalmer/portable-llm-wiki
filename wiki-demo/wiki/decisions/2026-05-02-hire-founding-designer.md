# 2026-05-02 Hire Founding Designer

Decided to extend an offer to [[Mia Patel]] as founding designer at
[[Strand Bio]].

## Context

The team had been deferring the design hire for three months. The CEO
wanted to wait for the seed round to close; [[Avery Chen]] wanted to
move sooner because the inventory service's UX was visibly held together
with engineering taste alone (a bad sign).

Three candidates made it to the final round. Mia was the second.

## Decision

Extend an offer to Mia. Start date 2026-05-16. Compensation at the
80th percentile of seed-stage founding-designer comp; 1.2% equity.

## Rationale

The three signals on Mia's [[Mia Patel]] page were dispositive. Of the
three finalists:

- Candidate A was technically strongest but couldn't articulate *why*
  any of their past designs worked the way they did. Failed the
  [[Provenance Over Recall]] filter.
- **Mia** was the strongest at the load-bearing skills for a 9-person
  team (see [[Small Teams Compounding]]). Could read code, took
  feedback well, and pushed back on weak ideas in interviews.
- Candidate C was strong on portfolio but visibly more excited by
  process-design than product-design. Wrong fit at this stage.

## Risk

Mia's last role was at a series-B with 80+ people. Going from 80 to 9
is a big step down in organizational complexity, and some designers
struggle when the meeting calendar disappears. The mitigation: paid
trial month before the formal start, structured around shipping one real
piece of the inventory service. Mia accepted the trial without
negotiation, which was itself a signal.

## Status

Trial month completed successfully on 2026-05-16. Mia is now full-time
on the team and is the design owner of the inventory service v1.
