# 2025-09-14 Joining Strand Bio

Decided to leave the medtech startup [[Avery Chen]] had been at for
two years and join [[Strand Bio]] as founding engineer (employee #2,
after [[Linh Park]]).

## Context

[[Linh Park]] sent a cold email on a Wednesday in late August 2025
referencing a specific paragraph in one of Avery's old PhD-era
preprints. The one closest to what would later become the inventory
service's provenance model. The email was three paragraphs, no
deck, no pitch, no calendly link. Just: "I'm starting a company
about this; coffee?"

Avery had been at the medtech startup for 22 months. The
work was good. The team was good. The product was visibly going to
plateau six months out. The addressable market topped out at the
hospital systems already on the customer list, and the next ring of
buyers wanted a fundamentally different product. Avery had been
quietly looking for the next thing for about ten weeks.

## Decision

Accept. Three-week notice period at the medtech job. Start date
2025-10-06 as employee #2.

## Rationale

Three reasons:

1. **The founder was right about the problem.** First coffee, Linh
   sketched the orchestration-layer gap (see
   [[Benchling]]) on a napkin and identified the exact mistake the
   previous generation of lab-software startups made. Avery had
   thought through the same map and converged on the same answer.
   This is rare and load-bearing.
2. **Founder + first-engineer fit was unusually clean.** Linh had
   strong opinions about what to build and weak opinions about how
   to build it; Avery is the inverse. The seam between "biologist
   PM" and "founding engineer" lines up cleanly.
3. **Optionality.** Two years from now, either the company is real
   (Avery is positioned as the technical founder of a synth-bio
   company at age 29) or it isn't (Avery is a strong founding
   engineer who has now run a real attempt. A much better
   credential than another 22 months of incremental medtech work).
   Both paths beat staying.

## Counterarguments considered

- *"Seed-stage equity at a synth-bio company is mostly worth zero."*
  Acknowledged. The decision was not made on equity math; it was made
  on the option value of the next two years.
- *"Boston is not where you live."* True. Avery negotiated remote
  for the first six months and relocated in March 2026 (see
  [[2026-04-30 Keep Team Onsite]]).
- *"You'll regret leaving a stable thing."* Symmetrical with the
  argument against [[2024-08-15 Leaving Grad School]]; rejected for
  the same reason.

## Compensation

Below-market base ($165k), 4.2% equity with a 4-year vest and a
1-year cliff, no signing bonus. Cliff hits 2026-10-06.

## Status

Eight months in. Avery has shipped the [[Strand Bio Inventory Service]]
to private beta with three customer labs and has hired
[[Mia Patel]]. The medtech job is, in retrospect, the last "safe"
role Avery will take.
