# Purpose

This wiki exists so [[Avery Chen]] doesn't have to re-explain themselves
every time a new LLM session starts, every time a new hire joins
[[Strand Bio]], and every time an investor asks a question they've
already answered three times.

It's a **Portable LLM Wiki**. Markdown files in a git repo, owned by
Avery, exposable to any LLM client (Cursor, Claude Desktop, ChatGPT,
local models) via either an HTTP API or a native MCP server.

## What goes in

- **Entities**: people, companies, products
- **Concepts**: operating principles, frameworks, ways of thinking
- **Decisions**: career or technical choices with explicit rationale
- **Projects**: what's currently shipping
- **Sources**: digests of the conversations, articles, and meetings the
  above were derived from

## What stays out

- Anything sensitive (medical, financial, family). Those go on `tier:
  private` pages or in a different system entirely.
- Speculation. Every claim cites a source.

## The bet

If the wiki compounds (a few new pages a week), in two years the answer
to *"who is Avery Chen?"* will be richer than any traditional resume
could be. And Avery will own it.
