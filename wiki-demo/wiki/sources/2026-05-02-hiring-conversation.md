# 2026-05-02 Hiring Conversation

Digest of the post-interview debrief between [[Avery Chen]] and the CEO
of [[Strand Bio]], the morning after [[Mia Patel]]'s final round.

## What's in it

The conversation that produced [[2026-05-02 Hire Founding Designer]].
Walks through all three finalists, why two were rejected, and what
specifically about Mia made the offer obvious.

## Anchor quotes

> "Mia read the schema. I don't know how to overstate how rare that is.
> Most designers wouldn't have opened the file."
> — Avery

> "When she pushed back on the receiving-doc layout, she was right and
> we were wrong. That's the hire."
> — CEO

## How this conversation changed the team

The debrief produced a hiring filter that the team has used twice since:
*can the candidate critique a load-bearing artifact from the team's
current work?* For engineers it's a PR; for designers it's a schema
or a flow doc. For a future PM hire it'll be a roadmap.
