# 2026-05-15 Building In Public Talk

Digest of [[Avery Chen]]'s 12-minute talk at a small Boston "building
in public" meetup. Topic: what working at a 9-person startup actually
looks like, vs. the Twitter version.

## What's in it

Three claims, each with a concrete example from [[Strand Bio]]:

1. The hardest part of a 9-person team isn't shipping fast. It's
   refusing to grow. ([[Small Teams Compounding]])
2. Most "founder fights" between technical and biz co-founders are
   actually fights about provenance under another name.
   ([[Provenance Over Recall]])
3. New-stack worship is the #1 cause of avoidable failure in YC-stage
   companies. Boring beats trendy at this scale.
   ([[Boring Stack First]])

## Anchor quote

> "We're 9 people. Every framework I add is a person I have to hire to
> babysit it. That's the only test that matters."
> — Avery

## Why it's worth keeping

This talk was the first time Avery articulated the three operating
principles in public, in front of an audience that wasn't predisposed
to agree. The Q&A pushback ("isn't this just engineering conservatism?")
sharpened the framing. See the cross-link from [[Boring Stack First]]
back to this source.
