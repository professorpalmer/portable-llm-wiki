# Purpose

This wiki exists so I don't have to re-explain myself every time a new
LLM session starts. It's a **Portable LLM Wiki**. Markdown files in a
git repo, owned by me, exposable to any LLM client (Cursor, Claude
Desktop, ChatGPT, local models) via HTTP or a native MCP server.

> Replace this body with your own framing. Why does *your* wiki exist?
> Who are you writing it for (future-you, recruiters, friends,
> collaborators)? What kinds of pages live here and what stays out?

## What goes in

- **Entities**: people, companies, products
- **Concepts**: operating principles, frameworks, ways of thinking
- **Decisions**: career or technical choices with explicit rationale
- **Projects**: what you're currently shipping
- **Sources**: digests of the conversations, articles, and meetings the
  above were derived from

## What stays out

- Anything sensitive lives on `tier: private` pages (or a different
  system entirely). The default tier on this wiki is `public`, so
  anything you don't explicitly mark stays visible.
- Speculation. Every claim should cite a source.
