# First Principle

> This is a placeholder for your first operating principle. A way of
> thinking or working that governs your decisions. Rename this file and
> rewrite this body to capture one such principle in your own words.

A good operating-principle page has:

1. **A crisp one-sentence statement** of the principle at the top.
2. **The *why***. What failure mode the principle protects against.
3. **How it's applied**. Concrete examples from your projects /
   decisions.
4. **When to break it**. Every rule has limits; documenting them keeps
   the principle honest.
